            - Exercise 1
                - Question
                    - Prompt

                        Some people argue that governments should invest more in public transport rather than building new roads. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

                    - Diagram Image Link

                        

                - Answer
                    - Introduction

                        It is often argued that state funds should be allocated towards improving public transport infrastructure instead of constructing more highways. I firmly agree with this statement, as investing in public transit is crucial for environmental sustainability and urban efficiency.

                    - Body 1

                        The most compelling reason for this view is the urgent need to reduce carbon emissions. Private vehicles are a major contributor to air pollution and global warming. By investing in efficient and affordable public transport networks, governments can encourage citizens to leave their cars at home. For instance, cities with extensive subway systems report significantly lower levels of urban air pollution. Consequently, a shift towards public transit is essential for a greener future.

                    - Body 2

                        Furthermore, building new roads often fails to solve traffic congestion in the long term. This phenomenon, known as induced demand, occurs when new highways simply encourage more people to drive, quickly leading to the same level of gridlock. Conversely, high-capacity public transport, such as trains and buses, can move large numbers of people efficiently without taking up excessive urban space. Therefore, prioritizing public transit creates more livable cities.

                    - Conclusion

                        In conclusion, I strongly believe that governments must prioritize public transport over road construction. This approach not only combats environmental degradation but also provides a more sustainable solution to urban traffic challenges.

                    - Diagram Image Link

                        

                - Answer
                    - Introduction

                        Urban areas worldwide are increasingly plagued by severe traffic congestion. This essay will examine the primary causes of this issue and propose viable solutions to alleviate the problem and improve city transportation.

                    - Body 1

                        The root cause of traffic congestion is the overreliance on private vehicles. As populations grow and urban sprawl increases, many people find public transport to be inadequate, unreliable, or overcrowded.                 - Answer
                    - Introduction

                        The transition from traditional office environments to remote working has accelerated rapidly in recent years. While there are certainly challenges associated with telecommuting, I firmly believe that the advantages significantly outweigh the disadvantages.

                    - Body 1
