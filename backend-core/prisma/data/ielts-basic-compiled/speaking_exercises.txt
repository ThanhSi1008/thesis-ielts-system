    - Best Response Practice
        - Part 1
            - Exercise 1
                - Question
                    - Prompt

                        Examiner asks: "Do you enjoy cooking?"

                    - QuestionType

                        mcq

                - Answer
                    - Options

                        A) Yes.
                        B) Yes, I love cooking. I cook every day. Cooking is my hobby.
                        C) Well, actually I'm quite passionate about cooking. I particularly enjoy experimenting with Thai cuisine because the combination of flavours is incredibly diverse.
                        D) I think cooking is very important for health.

                    - CorrectAnswer

                        C

                    - Feedback A

                        Too short — the examiner expects extended answers of 2-3 sentences.

                    - Feedback B

                        Repetitive — uses 'cooking' 3 times with no vocabulary variety.

                    - Feedback C

                        Excellent — extended, natural, uses range, gives a specific example.

                    - Feedback D

                        Doesn't directly answer whether YOU enjoy cooking.

            - Exercise 2
                - Question
                    - Prompt

                        Examiner asks: "Do you prefer reading books or watching movies?"

                    - QuestionType

                        mcq

                - Answer
                    - Options

                        A) I like movies because they are fun.
                        B) To be honest, I lean more towards watching movies. I find that immersing myself in a visually stunning film is the perfect way to unwind after a stressful day.
                        C) Books are good but movies are good too.
                        D) I don't know, maybe movies.

                    - CorrectAnswer

                        B

                    - Feedback A

                        Too basic — lacks vocabulary range and doesn't fully explain why.

                    - Feedback B

                        Excellent — uses a natural opener, good vocabulary ('lean towards', 'immersing myself', 'unwind'), and gives a clear reason.

                    - Feedback C

                        Too simple and repetitive — doesn't actually answer which one you prefer.

                    - Feedback D

                        Hesitant and underdeveloped — you must take a position and explain it.

                    - QuestionType

                        mcq

                - Answer
                    - Options

                        A) Yes, they should because it's good for their brain and makes them smart.
                        B) I think music is nice.
                        C) Absolutely. From my perspective, learning an instrument is highly beneficial. It not only fosters creativity but also teaches discipline, which are crucial skills for their future development.

                    - CorrectAnswer

                        C

                    - Feedback A

                        A bit too informal for Part 3 ('makes them smart') and the sentence structure is very basic.

                    - Feedback B

                        Fails to answer the specific question about children learning an instrument.

                    - Feedback C

                        Excellent — uses formal opinion language ('From my perspective'), advanced vocabulary ('fosters', 'crucial'), and a complex sentence structure ('not only... but also').

                    - Feedback D

                        Doesn't answer the question of whether they *should* learn an instrument.

            - Exercise 4
                - Question
                    - Prompt

                        Examiner asks: "Is it better to live in the city or the countryside?"

                    - QuestionType

                        mcq

                - Answer
                    - Options

                        A) City is better because it has many shops and restaurants.
                        B) I live in the city and I like it.
                        C) The countryside has fresh air.
                        D) That's an interesting question. I'd argue that it depends on your stage in life. While the city offers incredible career opportunities for young professionals, the countryside provides a tranquil environment that is often more suitable for raising a family.

                    - Feedback A

                        Answers the question but uses very basic vocabulary and simple sentence structure.

                    - Feedback B

                        Too personal — Part 3 is about society in general, not just your personal life.

                    - Feedback C

                        Only presents one side and fails to make a comparison or reach a conclusion.

                    - Feedback D

                        Excellent — uses a natural filler to buy time, presents a balanced view ('While...'), and uses high-level vocabulary ('tranquil', 'suitable').
