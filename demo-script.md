## 2. IELTS Writing (Cambridge 17 - Test 4)

### 2.1. Task 1

```text
The line graph illustrates the number of shop closures and new openings in a specific country over an eight-year period from 2011 to 2018.
Overall, both shop openings and closures experienced fluctuating trends, with both figures ending the period lower than their initial levels. Notably, while the number of newly opened shops started higher than closures, the latter dominated for the majority of the time frame, except for a dramatic drop in 2015.
In 2011, shop openings stood at their peak of approximately 8,500, significantly outnumbering closures which were at around 6,400. However, the number of new businesses plummeted to just 4,000 in 2012 before recovering steadily to reach 6,200 in 2014. In contrast, closures witnessed a minor dip to 6,000 in 2012, followed by a sharp increase to a peak of about 7,200 in 2013, before dropping slightly to 6,500 in 2014.
The year 2015 saw a dramatic shift; shop closures plunged to an all-time low of roughly 500, while openings also fell to 4,000. Following this anomaly, closures surged back up to 5,200 in 2016 and remained relatively stable until 2018. Meanwhile, the number of new openings hovered around 4,000 until 2017, before experiencing a final decline to 3,000 at the end of the period.
```

### 2.2. Task 2

```text
In recent years, an increasing number of individuals suffering from various health issues have opted for alternative therapies, such as herbal medicine and acupuncture, rather than consulting conventional medical practitioners. Although this trend reflects a growing desire for natural and holistic well-being, I argue that entirely replacing professional medical advice with unverified alternative treatments is a profoundly negative development.
On the one hand, the appeal of alternative medicine is understandable, as it often provides a more holistic and less invasive approach to health. Many traditional pharmaceuticals come with a barrage of side effects, leading patients to seek natural remedies. For chronic conditions such as mild arthritis, stress, or insomnia, therapies like massage, yoga, or herbal teas can alleviate symptoms effectively without the risk of chemical dependency. Furthermore, alternative medicine often emphasizes lifestyle modifications and preventative care, empowering individuals to take active control of their long-term health rather than simply reacting to symptoms.
However, substituting qualified medical doctors with alternative practitioners poses severe, life-threatening risks. The primary danger lies in the lack of rigorous scientific validation and regulation governing many alternative therapies. Unlike conventional medicine, which undergoes decades of stringent clinical trials, many alternative treatments rely on anecdotal evidence, which can lead to ineffective care or dangerous toxic interactions. More critically, avoiding standard medical check-ups often results in misdiagnosis or delayed treatment for acute and progressive illnesses such as cancer or cardiovascular diseases. By the time a patient realizes that alternative therapies are failing, their condition may have deteriorated to an untreatable stage.
In conclusion, while alternative treatments can serve as a beneficial complement to standard medical care for minor ailments, using them as a total replacement for conventional healthcare is highly perilous. Therefore, the growing tendency to bypass regular doctors in favor of unverified alternative remedies is ultimately a negative development.
```

## 3. Cấu hình Card Type (Vocab Lab)

Cấu trúc các trường thông tin hiển thị ở các mặt thẻ ghi nhớ:

- **Mặt trước (Front):**
  - `word (the target word to study)`
- **Mặt sau (Back):**
  - `Meaning (definition and translation of the word)`, `Example (an illustrative sentence showing context)`, `Collocations (common word pairings)`, `IELTS Relevance (how the word relates to the IELTS exam)`
