## 1. AI Writing Grader (Đánh giá viết tự động)
Dành cho chức năng chấm điểm Writing (Task 1 & Task 2) tích hợp AI. Sử dụng các đề thi trong bộ đề Cambridge IELTS 17 đã được seed sẵn trong database.

### 📝 Đề 1: Cambridge IELTS 17 - Writing Test 1

#### Task 1: Norbiton Town Future Development (Academic Map)
* **Đề bài (Prompt):**
  ```text
  The maps below show an industrial area in the town of Norbiton, and planned future development of the site. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.
  ```

* **Bài viết mẫu Band 8.5+ (High-Score - Copy để demo kết quả xuất sắc):**
  ```text
  The maps illustrate the structural changes that are proposed to take place in the industrial town of Norbiton. Overall, it is planned that the entire industrial zone will be converted into a residential area, marked by a massive expansion of housing and the introduction of new public facilities.

  To begin with, the current layout of Norbiton consists mostly of factories situated along a main road running from west to east, with a roundabout at the eastern end. A river lies to the north of the industrial zone.

  Under the redevelopment plan, all existing factories will be demolished to make way for housing. A new roundabout will be constructed in the center of the town, connecting to the eastern roundabout via a new road. Surrounding this new central roundabout, a series of residential houses will be built, with additional housing units placed to the north and west of the area. 

  Furthermore, several social infrastructures are planned. A playground will be built in the eastern section, while a medical center and a school will be established in the southeastern and northern parts respectively. To cross the river, a new bridge will be constructed, providing access to another newly developed housing area on the opposite bank.
  ```

* **Bài viết mẫu Band 5.5 - 6.0 (Average - Copy để demo tính năng chỉ lỗi sai và góp ý sửa lỗi của AI):**
  ```text
  The maps show how an industrial area in Norbiton town will look in the future. We can see that there are many factories now, but in the future they will change to houses. 

  At the moment, the industrial area has some factories next to a road. The road runs from west to east, and there is a roundabout on the right side. There is a river at the top of the map. 

  In the future, the factories will be gone. Instead, many houses will be build. They will make a new roundabout in the middle of the area, and it connects to the old roundabout. People will build houses around the new roads. There will also be a school and a playground, and a medical centre. A new bridge will be build to go across the river, and there will be more houses on the other side of the river. 

  Generally, the area will change a lot and become a place for people to live instead of factories.
  ```

#### Task 2: Taking Risks in Life (Opinion Essay)
* **Đề bài (Prompt):**
  ```text
  It is important for people to take risks, both in their professional lives and their personal lives. Do you think the advantages of taking risks outweigh the disadvantages? Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.
  ```

* **Bài viết mẫu Band 8.0+ (High-Score):**
  ```text
  It is often argued that taking risks in both professional and personal spheres is essential for individuals. In my opinion, while there are minor drawbacks associated with potential failures, the benefits of embracing risks, such as personal growth and career advancement, far outweigh the disadvantages.

  On the one hand, the primary disadvantage of risk-taking is the possibility of failure and the subsequent negative consequences. In professional life, venturing into an unstable business market or leaving a secure job to start a new business can lead to financial ruin. For instance, many startups fail within their first few years, leaving founders in debt. In personal lives, taking physical risks, like extreme sports, can result in severe injuries, while emotional risks, like relocating to a new country, may lead to isolation and stress. Therefore, risk-taking can sometimes compromise an individual's security and well-being.

  On the other hand, I believe that the advantages of taking risks are significantly more profound. Firstly, calculated risks are the cornerstone of career development. Employees who volunteer for challenging projects or pivot to new industries often acquire rare skills and secure promotions much faster than those who remain in their comfort zones. Secondly, on a personal level, risk-taking fosters resilience and self-confidence. Even when risks do not yield the desired outcome, the experience gained from navigating uncertainty is invaluable. For example, learning to adapt to a foreign culture after moving abroad builds global awareness and independence. Without taking these leaps of faith, individuals would remain stagnant.

  In conclusion, although taking risks can sometimes result in failure or instability, the positive outcomes in terms of professional progression and personal enrichment are far more significant. Hence, people should be encouraged to take calculated risks to reach their full potential.
  ```

* **Bài viết mẫu Band 5.5 - 6.0 (Average):**
  ```text
  Many people think that taking risks in their work and life is very important. I agree with this because taking risks can bring many advantages, although there are also some disadvantages.

  The main disadvantage of taking risks is that you can fail. For example, if you leave your job to start a new company, you might lose all your money because many businesses fail. Also, in personal life, if you take risks like moving to another country, you can feel lonely and miss your family. This is very bad for your health and mind.

  However, I believe that the advantages are more important. First, you can get a better job or more money if you take risks. People who try new things at work can learn more and get promoted. Second, taking risks makes you stronger. If you never try anything new, your life will be boring and you cannot learn from your mistakes. For example, my friend moved to another city for university, and even though it was hard, he became very independent and made many new friends.

  To conclude, you can fail when taking risks and it can be dangerous, but it is still good because it helps you grow in your life and career. Therefore, the advantages are stronger than the disadvantages.
  ```

## 2. AI Speaking Grader (Đánh giá nói tự động)
Dữ liệu chữ (transcripts) để người dùng có thể tự đọc vào mic hoặc dán vào ô test trong trường hợp cần nhập text/transcripts mẫu cho tính năng Speaking.

### 🎙️ Đề 1: Cambridge IELTS 17 - Speaking Test 1

#### Part 1: History
* **Q1:** *What did you study in history lessons when you were at school?*
  * **Sample Answer (Tự nhiên, lưu loát):**
    > "Well, when I was at school, our history curriculum was quite broad. We studied national history, focusing on the country's struggle for independence and major dynasties. Additionally, we touched upon key global events like World War I and II, which I found particularly fascinating."
* **Q2:** *Did you enjoy studying history at school? Why, or why not?*
  * **Sample Answer (Tự nhiên, lưu loát):**
    > "To be honest, it was a bit of a mixed bag. I really enjoyed learning about the historical context and the stories behind major events. However, I wasn't a big fan of memorizing exact dates and statistics for exams, which felt rather tedious."
* **Q3:** *How often do you watch TV programmes about history now? Why, or why not?*
  * **Sample Answer:**
    > "I wouldn't say I watch them on a daily basis, but occasionally, maybe once or twice a week, I'll tune in to a historical documentary on platforms like Netflix or YouTube, especially those about ancient civilizations or mysteries."
* **Q4:** *What period in history would you like to learn more about? Why?*
  * **Sample Answer:**
    > "I've always had a keen interest in the Renaissance period in Europe. The sheer explosion of art, science, and philosophical thinking during that era is incredibly inspiring, and I'd love to delve deeper into how it shaped the modern world."

#### Part 2: Cue Card
* **Topic:** *Describe the neighbourhood you lived in when you were a child.*
* **Sample Response (Band 7.5+, ~1.5 phút đọc):**
  ```text
  I would like to talk about the neighbourhood where I spent most of my childhood. It was a relatively quiet residential area located in the suburban part of Hanoi, about 10 kilometres from the city centre. 

  Most of the residents in my neighbourhood were middle-class families, and many of them had lived there for decades. The community was highly diverse, consisting of teachers, office workers, and retirees. Everyone was incredibly friendly and welcoming. 

  Living in that neighbourhood was an absolute joy. Since there was very little traffic, my friends and I could play traditional games in the alleyways or ride our bicycles around the blocks without our parents having to worry about safety. There was a real sense of community; people left their doors open, and neighbours would frequently drop by for a cup of tea or share home-cooked meals. 

  If you ask me whether I would like to live there in the future, my answer would be yes, but with some reservations. On one hand, I deeply miss the warmth and the tight-knit relationships of that community, which are hard to find in modern apartment complexes. On the other hand, the area lacks modern facilities like large supermarkets or gyms. However, for raising children, I still think it would be an ideal place.
  ```

#### Part 3: Discussion (Neighbours & Cities)
* **Q1:** *What sort of things can neighbours do to help each other?*
  * **Sample Answer:**
    > "There are numerous ways neighbours can assist one another. On a basic level, they can keep an eye on each other’s houses when one is away, which enhances security. In times of emergency, such as a power outage or a medical crisis, a close neighbour is often the first person who can provide immediate support."
* **Q2:** *How well do people generally know their neighbours in your country?*
  * **Sample Answer:**
    > "It varies significantly between rural and urban areas. In countryside communities, relationships are close-knit, and everyone knows each other by name. In contrast, in major cities and apartment buildings, people tend to lead very private lives, and it's quite common to only exchange polite nods in the elevator."

## 4. Pronunciation (Luyện phát âm chuẩn IPA)
Các câu tiếng Anh chứa các âm IPA đặc trưng được seed trong hệ thống để demo tính năng đánh giá phát âm qua mic (Pronunciation Check).

### 🎯 Nhóm Nguyên âm (Vowels)
* **Âm /i:/ (sheep) vs /ɪ/ (ship):**
  * `The sheep on the ship is eating green grass.`
* **Âm /u:/ (food) vs /ʊ/ (good):**
  * `The good food in the group was very sweet.`
* **Âm /æ/ (cat) vs /ʌ/ (up):**
  * `A black cat ran up the stairs to get the cup.`

### 🎯 Nhóm Phụ âm khó (Consonants)
* **Âm /θ/ (voiceless th - think) vs /ð/ (voiced th - this):**
  * `This mother thinks that three months is enough.`
* **Âm /ʃ/ (shall) vs /s/ (see):**
  * `She sells seashells by the seashore.`
* **Âm /tʃ/ (cheese) vs /dʒ/ (june):**
  * `A large child was watching television while eating cheese in June.`

## 5. Vocab Lab (Flashcards & Spaced Repetition)
Dữ liệu mẫu để tạo nhanh các Flashcards mới trong Vocab Lab.

### 🗂️ Deck Name: IELTS Academic Vocabulary
* **Card 1:**
  * **Front/Word:** `Exacerbate`
  * **IPA:** `/ɪɡˈzæsəbeɪt/`
  * **Meaning:** Làm trầm trọng hơn (to make a problem, bad situation, or negative feeling worse).
  * **Example:** `The cold weather exacerbated her cough.`
* **Card 2:**
  * **Front/Word:** `Unprecedented`
  * **IPA:** `/ʌnˈpresɪdentɪd/`
  * **Meaning:** Chưa từng có tiền lệ (never done or known before).
  * **Example:** `The Internet has given people unprecedented access to information.`
* **Card 3:**
  * **Front/Word:** `Substantiate`
  * **IPA:** `/səbˈstænʃieɪt/`
  * **Meaning:** Chứng minh, xác minh (provide evidence to support or prove the truth of).
  * **Example:** `They had found no evidence to substantiate the allegations.`
