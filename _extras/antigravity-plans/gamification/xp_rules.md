# Lexon XP & Leveling Guide

## How Leveling Works

Lexon uses a progressive leveling system where each consecutive level takes more XP to complete. 

**Rule:** To complete a level and move to the next, you need `100 × (Next Level)` XP.
- **Level 1:** 100 XP
- **Level 2:** 200 XP
- **Level 3:** 300 XP
- **Level 4:** 400 XP

*Your **Total XP** is the cumulative sum of all XP you've ever earned. The progress bar in your profile shows how much XP you have earned towards your **next** level.*

---

## How to Earn XP

You earn XP automatically by completing activities across the app. Here is the exact breakdown of how much XP every action gives you:

### 📖 Foundation Modules
- **Vocabulary:** Complete a vocabulary unit = **15 XP**
- **Grammar:** Complete a grammar unit = **15 XP**
- **Pronunciation:** Practice a sound = **5 XP**
- **Pronunciation:** Master a sound = **10 XP**

### 🎧 Shadowing & Dictation
- **Dictation:** Type a sentence correctly = **2 XP** (per sentence)
- **Dictation:** Complete a full dictation lesson = **15 XP**
- **Shadowing:** Speak a sentence correctly = **2 XP** (per sentence)
- **Shadowing:** Complete a full shadowing lesson = **15 XP**

### 📚 Vocab Lab (Flashcards)
- **Review Card:** Study and review a card = **2 XP**
- **Graduate Card:** Move a card out of the learning phase = **5 XP**
- **Publish Deck:** Share your deck with the community = **15 XP**

### 🚀 IELTS Courses
- **IELTS Basic:** Complete a lesson = **10 XP**
- **IELTS Basic:** Complete an exercise (Reading/Listening/Writing) = **15 XP**
- **IELTS Advanced:** Complete an Advanced Listening/Reading Session = **20 XP**
- **IELTS Intensive:** Submit an Intensive Exam section = **20 XP**

### 💬 Community
- **Create a Post:** = **5 XP**
- **Write a Comment:** = **2 XP**

### 🔥 Daily Streaks
- **Streak Bonus:** Logging in and maintaining your streak gives you **5 XP × Your Current Streak**!
  *(Example: A 5-day streak gives you 25 XP that day. A 10-day streak gives you 50 XP!)*

---

### 🏆 Achievements
In addition to the daily actions above, you also earn **bonus XP** when you unlock specific achievements (e.g., *Vocab Bookworm*, *Grammar Graduate*, etc.). Checking your profile's achievement tab will show you specific badge requirements.
